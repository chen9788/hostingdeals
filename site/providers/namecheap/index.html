ICANN fee: added separately

Namecheap lists .com, .net and .org as affected extensions. Its policy specifies USD 0.20 per year for applicable registrations, renewals and transfers, added at purchase and separately itemized in the cart. Source: Namecheap ICANN fee policy, checked 2026-10-12.

“the ICANN fee will be added to the listed price for the domain at the time of purchase”

The renewal total above is arithmetic from the published price and fee, not a checkout test or an all-in tax quote.

Included services and separately priced extras

Eligible domains include free lifetime domain privacy. Domain (URL) forwarding and email forwarding are free when the domain uses Namecheap default DNS; these services do not work with custom nameservers. Free forwarding is not a full email mailbox or an outgoing-email service. Private Email, hosting, SSL and PremiumDNS are optional separate products; they are not included in the domain price shown here.

Sources: Official domain privacy policy, official default DNS and free-services documentation, official free email forwarding limitations and official .com add-on products — checked 2026-10-12.

Promotion windows: checked separately from prices

This update adds window information that was not verified in the earlier snapshot above. For the USD 6.79 new-customer price, the official .com page restricts eligibility to the first registration year and one domain per user or household, and describes the offer as limited-time. No fixed end date was found for this offer in the inspected official page. No evidence of a shared stock or campaign quota ending the offer was found; the per-customer purchase limit is not a campaign expiry.

Source: official .com promotion terms — checked 2026-10-12.

For the USD 10.98 first-year birthday registration price, the official birthday page gives conflicting deadlines. Its FAQ says the registration and transfer offers run from midnight October 6 through October 14 at 11:59 PM ET; its General terms instead say the promotional prices are available from October 6 through October 12 at 11:59 PM ET. These are the 2026 campaign dates as identified on the same page. The exact applicable end date for this registration price is therefore unresolved; confirm with Namecheap before relying on either deadline.

Official deadline excerpts: FAQ “October 14, 11:59 PM ET”; General terms “October 12, 11:59 PM ET”. Source: Namecheap birthday campaign, FAQ and General terms — checked 2026-10-12. Neither window changes the standard renewal price documented above.
