OUR STANDARD

Evidence before a headline.

Official sources, observable fields, explicit gaps.

What “observed” means

A deterministic parser fetched the official page and matched the offer and its required restrictions. It does not mean a coupon passed checkout. We do not create accounts or test purchases.

Region & currency

This is an English-language site. The monitor requests en-US, rejects configured non-US redirects and retains price symbols exactly as displayed. A dollar symbol alone does not establish a currency code or eligibility in your country. Check the linked source and checkout.

Dates & failure handling

Checked dates are crawl times, not offer start dates. Missing start, expiry, currency or renewal fields remain unknown. Offers older than 48 hours, failed extractions and expired offers are excluded from the current list when the site is rebuilt. Archived pages retain their original verification date. If updates stop, refer to that date before relying on an offer.

Sources & independence

We use official public pages, honor robots rules, and do not bypass TLS checks, logins or anti-bot challenges. Unknown markup is suppressed. Sitemap discovery helps identify potential sources; it does not prove an offer.

Pricing context

Monthly introductory rates can require full-term prepayment. Taxes, extras, renewals and overages may be additional. Compare the total commitment, not just the monthly headline.
