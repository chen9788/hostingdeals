Does switching from monthly to yearly Hostinger hosting give me a free domain?

No under Hostinger's documented billing-cycle rule: changing an existing monthly hosting plan to yearly does not qualify for a free domain. The official billing-cycle guide explicitly excludes that switch. Official review: October 23, 2026. Our account-level switch and domain-claim test remain not verified.

A new annual purchase and a billing-cycle change are different

It separately excludes monthly plans, renewals, upgrades and billing-cycle changes. A yearly billing selection alone therefore does not establish the new-purchase benefit.
