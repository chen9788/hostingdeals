Will transferring a domain to Namecheap add one year, and when will it not?

Usually yes for eligible generic TLDs, but the extension and recent expiry history matter. Namecheap says most generic TLDs renew during transfer and that “the time is added to the current expiration date of the domain.” This is an official rule, not our completed-transfer result. Official additional-year explanation, reviewed October 25, 2026.

The 45-day wording needs care. The transfer landing page describes renewal within the last 45 days and limits the warning to a domain that actually expired before renewal. The detailed help article and transfer guide say 45 days from the previous expiration date. These are the official pages' different wordings; we have not tested a boundary case and do not infer that every recent renewal loses a year. A transfer eligibility lock is a separate check. The official guide says generic domains registered or transferred between registrars within the last 60 days cannot be transferred; country-code requirements may differ. This is not the same rule as the 45-day post-expiry warning. Official eligibility instructions.
