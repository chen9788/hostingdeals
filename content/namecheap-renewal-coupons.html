Namecheap renewal promo code: what is actually eligible?

Do not assume a Namecheap registration coupon works for a renewal: eligibility depends on the product and the specific promotion. Namecheap describes its monthly domain coupons as registration and transfer offers, its regular hosting-renewal guide excludes promotion codes, and its automatic-billing guide says codes are not applied automatically. What to record before relying on a renewal discount

  1. Identify the exact renewing product and, for domains, the extension. A domain transfer and renewal are different transactions.
  2. Keep the official campaign URL, its dates and its renewal-specific wording together. A general coupon headline is insufficient.
  3. Record the renewal period, currency and amount label. Keep a published price separate from an account quote and a final bill.
  4. Record whether billing is automatic or manual; automatic billing does not apply promo codes automatically under the cited guide.
  5. Only an authorized, matching renewal checkout response can establish that order's acceptance. We have not performed that test.
