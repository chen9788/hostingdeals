Does a Cloudways card-verification charge prove my coupon worked?

No. A payment-method check and a promotional discount answer different questions. The official account-upgrade guide, reviewed 2026-11-26, describes a temporary $1 credit/debit-card authorization that is reversed after successful authorization. Its PayPal example is different: $5 is applied toward an outstanding balance or a later invoice. Neither amount is a coupon saving.
